ゲレンデの記憶

A Heartless Woman
大森 カエ
こんな僕で、ごめんね〜I cannot live on my own.〜 Ⅱ
Verse 1

友達と 共に笑い 過ごした日々 今は 年数回 話すだけ The days we spent laughing together with friends — now, we only talk a few times a year.

Verse 2

あの子が 悪かったと 周りは言う 避けれなかった あの瞬間 "That girl was at fault," everyone around says — that moment I couldn't avoid.

Pre-chorus

慣れ親しんだ ゲレンデ 綺麗な 雪の世界 The familiar ski slope — a beautiful world of snow.

Chorus 1

俺か好き そう言った彼女 今は人妻 A heartless woman 何も言わず 身を引いた 親友だった あいつは 目を伏せた それが答え "She liked me," she said. Now she's someone's wife. A heartless woman. Without a word, she stepped away. My best friend — he lowered his eyes. That was the answer.

Verse 3

感覚失った体 定期的に 体の向き変え 床ずれ予防 A body that lost all sensation. Regularly, turning my body over, to prevent bedsores.

Bridge 1

諦めなければ 辛いだけ いっそ… If I don't give up, it's only painful. I might as well…

Chorus 2

生きる意味 探し続ける 呼吸するだけ I can't even eat by myself. 真夜中 目を覚まし 過去を思い出す 時間が 巻き戻ったら 塩水が 頬伝る The meaning of living, I keep searching for it — just breathing. I can't even eat by myself. In the middle of the night, I wake up and remember the past. If time could be rewound — salty water runs down my cheek.

Bridge 2

眩い雪 心踊った あの頃 The dazzling snow — my heart used to dance, back then.

Outro

飛び出した 少女 あの瞬間 死を意識した なのに… 生き延びた事が 良かったのか 答えを探している A girl who darted out — that moment, I became aware of death. And yet… was the fact that I survived a good thing? I'm still searching for the answer.

Still searching for the answer. 離れていく人がいた。それでも、離れずにいてくれる人もいた。
