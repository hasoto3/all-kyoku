ごめんね、それしかできない

All I Can Do Is Laugh
大森 カエ
こんな僕で、ごめんね〜I cannot live on my own.〜 Ⅲ
Verse 1

動けなくなり 親が泣いた 仕事を辞めて 僕の 世話してくれた I became unable to move, and my parent cried. They quit their job and took care of me.

Verse 2

寝たきりになり 弟は 僕を避けた 一人 泣いた日々 Bedridden, my younger brother avoided me. Alone, I spent days crying.

Pre-chorus

若いからと 施設に入れず 自宅介護 Because I'm young, they wouldn't put me in a facility — caring for me at home.

Chorus 1

ごめんね 心で呟き All I can do is laugh. 生き残った 僕の精一杯 それしか できない 何も返せない 迷惑かけるだけ I'm sorry, I whisper it in my heart. All I can do is laugh. Having survived, this is my utmost effort — that's all I can do. I can't give anything back, I only cause trouble.

Bridge

働く事も 着替える事も トイレも 一人で出来ない Working, getting dressed, using the toilet — none of it, I can do alone.

Outro

情けなくて 涙も枯れた 生きている意味 探し続ける僕 It's so pitiful, even my tears have dried up. The meaning of being alive — I keep searching for it.

All I can do is laugh. 返せないことは、いる意味がないことじゃない。
誰かの手を借りて生きることに、謝らなくていい日が来ますように。
